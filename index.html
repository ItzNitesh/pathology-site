About Us

Revolutionizing Lab and Hospital Management Since 2002

SaralPro Technologies Pvt. Ltd. began its journey in 2002 with a vision to empower small and medium healthcare organizations through technology. In 2005, we introduced “Saral” — inspired by the Sanskrit word for simple and easy — reflecting our mission to simplify complex healthcare operations. Today, Saral Path is a trusted brand in the healthcare IT industry, offering modern, integrated solutions to hospitals and pathology labs across India. With over 2500+ satisfied clients, we continue to deliver efficient, secure, and user-friendly solutions that redefine lab automation.
